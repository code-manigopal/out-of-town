# Out of Town: Red Lake

2026-10-20 · Mani

## Overview

Part 2 of *Out of Town*: Nila is dying of a cancer Hades gave her on purpose, so Mani and his wife Preethi drive five days from Windsor to Red Lake, in Ontario's far north-west, to take the cure from him, with Nila guiding them by radio from her hospital bed.

**Premise.** Eleven months after Greyhaven. Mani has married Preethi and works as a data analytics engineer. Nila is back on air in Windsor. In early October she vomits blood at work and is rushed to hospital. The diagnosis is a rare, aggressive cancer. The oncologist calls it "atypical": it doesn't behave like anything he has seen. Nila, with her criminology training, puts it together first: the Old Merrow dive, and the water bottles left in the Pike Point shed that Jonah never brought.

**Hades's reason (the big reveal).** Mark Hayden, "Hades", runs Northstar Biologics, a secret lab in a closed gold mine outside Red Lake. Its product is **HX-7**, a peptide drug built from golden lancehead venom. One version of HX-7 causes a fast cancer. The other cures it. Whoever controls both controls who lives. Victor's smuggling paid for the research. Nila was poisoned for three reasons:

1. **Test subject:** she is Subject GH-01, the first human dose of the toxic version.
2. **Bait:** Hades knew Mani would come for the cure, and wants back the encrypted file Mani took from the mill.
3. **Revenge:** she cost him Greyhaven and his partner.

**How Part 2 plays**

- **8 episodes, 3.5 to 4 hours** in total, same rhythm as Part 1.
- **Two playable characters.** Mani and Preethi. The player switches between them in some scenes, and in Episodes 7 and 8 they split up and fight on separate paths.
- **Nila is the handler.** From a private radio set up in her hospital room and her apartment, she reads evidence, profiles suspects and plans routes. Mani and Preethi report to her every night. On air they speak Tanglish as a code, which Hades's men can't follow. Every line has English subtitles.
- **Nila's health** is a story clock. She gets weaker each episode. In Episode 6 she goes silent for a whole day.
- **Episodes 1 and 2 are the road trip:** 5 days, 3 motel nights, Tom Portons stops, small mysteries and new characters. **The investigation starts in Episode 3.**
- **Weapons:** one pump-action shotgun and one M4 rifle, swappable between the two; a Desert Eagle each. Grenade cases are found during the game and kept in the car trunk, so both always carry a few grenades.
- **Combat styles,** inspired by stealth-action games like *Hitman*: stealth (disguises, distractions, silent takedowns, avoiding detection) or aggressive (loud, fast, grenades). Most fights can be played either way.

## Red Lake: real places and fictional sites

Red Lake is a gold-mining municipality of about 4,000 people at the end of Highway 105, made up of Red Lake, Balmertown, Cochenour, McKenzie Island, Madsen and Starratt-Olsen (Back Roads Bill, SooToday). As in Part 1, real places set the scene and every crime happens at a fictional site.

**Real places used**

| Place | What it is | Use in the game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Highway 105 and Ear Falls | The only road into Red Lake | Ep 2 arrival drive, Ep 8 escape |
| Norseman monument, Red Lake waterfront | A floatplane replica on a pedestal; Red Lake calls itself the "Norseman Capital of the World" | First sight of town; meeting point with Daniel Keeper |
| Red Lake Margaret Cochenour Memorial Hospital | The town hospital | Ep 3: Dr. Marchetti and the matching cancers |
| Red Lake Regional Heritage Centre | Local history museum | Ep 3: the Hayden family's gold-rush history |
| Dickenson Mine viewing lot, Balmertown | Overlook of a historic mine painted by A.Y. Jackson | Ep 3: first look at the mining landscape |
| Erle Crull Rock and Mineral Collection, Balmertown Municipal Office | Over 2,600 specimens, including real gold | Ep 3 puzzle: a mislabelled "Hayden Deep" sample |
| Cochenour | Village on the lake | Ep 4: Sully's family, the ice road start |
| McKenzie Island | About 70 residents; ferry in summer, ice road in winter | Ep 4: Sully's cabin; night crossing on the ice road |
| Starratt-Olsen | A near-ghost town | Ep 6: ambush and rescue |
| Madsen | Old mining village | Ep 5: approach to the fictional mine |
| Red Lake airport and floatplane bases | Bush planes north | Ep 6 flight; Ep 8 escape |
| Spirit Rock (Medicine Rock) | A glacial erratic sacred to the Oji-Cree | One quiet, respectful moment only; never a crime site |
| Woodland Caribou Provincial Park | Vast wilderness west of town | Seen in the distance; the forest of Ep 7 lies on fictional land beyond it |

**Fictional sites**

| Site | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Northern Star Motel** | Bev's 12-room motel on the edge of town |
| **Hayden Deep Mine** | A gold mine closed in 1991, bought by Northstar Biologics. Above ground: a "mineral research" office. Underground: the venom farm and HX-7 lab. |
| **The Burn** | A private, fenced forest of about 400 km² north-west of town, burned in a 1990s fire and regrown thick. Patrolled by Kane's men. |
| **The Hades Mansion** | The Hayden estate on a frozen private lake at the far edge of the Burn: a stone-and-glass house, heated conservatories, animal enclosures. |
| **The Smoke** | A steaming, sulphur-smelling cone of black rock behind the mansion that locals call "the volcano". Nobody goes near the forest because of it. |

**About the volcano.** Ontario has no active volcanoes. Red Lake's rocks are ancient volcanic rock, around 2.7 to 2.9 billion years old (Wikipedia). The game can lean on that: geologists say the Smoke is impossible, but it steams anyway. One option is to explain it as an old mine fire burning underground. Another is to leave it a mystery for Part 3.

**Nila's home radio station.** Before they leave, Jonah drives down from Greyhaven and helps Nila set up a ham base station: a rig on her hospital bedside table (with the hospital's quiet permission) and a larger one with a roof antenna at her apartment for when she is discharged. **Grenades.** None are bought. The first grenade case is taken from Kane's men's truck in Episode 2. More are found in Episodes 5, 6 and 7. They live in the car trunk, and both characters always carry a few.

## Episode 1 · Lighthouse

Nila collapses, the cancer is diagnosed, a night raid on the Greyhaven mill ruins points to Red Lake, and Mani and Preethi set out north. **Covers:** the diagnosis, the Greyhaven trip, and travel days 1 and 2 (Windsor to Sudbury to Wawa). **Motel nights:** Sudbury, Wawa.

1. **Cold open, Windsor radio studio, 23:40.** Nila hosts her new late show on a Windsor station. Mid-sentence she coughs, tastes blood, and keeps talking until the song starts. Then she collapses onto the console. The on-air light stays red.
2. **Hospital, Windsor.** Mani and Preethi run through the emergency department. Days pass in a montage of scans. The oncologist: an aggressive, atypical cancer, "like nothing in the literature". Preethi, the pharmacist, reads the blood panel and goes quiet: one marker makes no sense. Amma and Appa land from Chennai. Tanglish scene at the bedside.
3. **Nila works it out.** From her bed, Nila talks through it like a case: "Exposure. Opportunity. Motive." The Old Merrow dive, and the Pike Point shed. She radios Jonah. Jonah confirms what he said last year: he never brought water. **Nila:** "Someone wanted me to drink that water, Anna."
4. **Night drive to Greyhaven (playable).** Mani and Preethi drive the county roads again. The first Tom Portons stop: Harold and June recognise Mani. Ruth meets them at the burned-out mill fence, older, still with the shotgun, still with the scanner.
5. **Stealth: the mill ruins.** The tutorial for switching between two characters. Preethi keeps watch from the water tower and spots the security patrol (the mill is now a police-sealed site with a private guard). Mani gets into Victor's old office. In the fireproof safe, which survived the fire: a Northstar Biologics shipping label, *"HX-7 — HAYDEN DEEP, RED LAKE, ON"*, and a delivery note for "24 × spring water, Pike Point, attn. GH-01", dated the week Nila hid there.
6. **Back in Windsor: Nila reads the clue.** Nila profiles Hades from what they bring: wealthy, patient, a collector, controlling, a family name in Red Lake's gold history. She lays out a plan. She sets up her radio station with Jonah. Call signs: Lighthouse, Anna, Anni. Appa insists on going north himself; Mani refuses. Amma presses a box of puliyodharai into Preethi's hands.
7. **Day 1 morning: shopping in Windsor.** The playable gear sequence (see the shopping list), ending with the trunk-packing puzzle.
8. **Day 1, Highway 401 and Highway 400 north.** A Tom Portons stop near Parry Sound. **Small mystery: "The Missing Laptop Bag."** A woman's bag has vanished from her booth. Clues: wet tyre tracks, a teen's guilty face, the bag's GPS tag on the woman's phone. The owner is **Ivy Laurent**. She thanks them a little too carefully and leaves heading north.
9. **Night 1: motel in Sudbury.** The Big Nickel lit up in the distance. **Small mystery: "The Uninvited Passenger."** Mani's phone keeps warning about an unknown Bluetooth tracker travelling with them. Search the car (wheel wells, bumper, under the seats). It's in the spare tyre. The player chooses: smash it, or (the smart choice) stick it on a transport truck heading east to Montreal.
10. **Day 2: Sudbury to Sault Ste. Marie to Wawa.** Lake Superior's north shore opens up: huge, grey, empty. A Tom Portons in Sault Ste. Marie: **small mystery: "The Tip Jar."** It's quick and funny, and teaches Preethi's observation skill. At the giant Wawa goose statue, Ivy is there again. Not a coincidence.
11. **Night 2: motel in Wawa.** Snow. In Ivy's room, the reveal: she is the Toronto journalist Nila emailed last year. Before journalism she was a lab technician at Northstar's Red Lake mine. She quit when she saw what HX-7 did and has been hiding since 2024. She draws them a rough map of the surface buildings. Nila, on the radio, questions Ivy like a detective and decides to trust her, for now.
12. **Cliffhanger.** 02:00. The car radio, on their private frequency, crackles. A calm voice, the one from Greyhaven:

**HADES (ON RADIO):** Welcome to the north, Mani. Bring the file. And tell your sister: GH-01 is doing very well.

## Episode 2 · Highway 17

The second half of the road trip: a light stop in White River, a night with Nila's old professor in Thunder Bay, the first gunfight in a snowstorm near Ignace, a sabotage mystery in Dryden, and arrival in Red Lake. **Covers:** travel days 3 to 5. **Motel night:** Dryden (the third motel). **Other night:** Thunder Bay, at Dr. Okafor's house.

1. **Day 3, Wawa to White River.** Ivy drives ahead in her own car; they keep radio contact. White River, the real hometown of the bear that became Winnie-the-Pooh. **Small mystery: "Winnie's Scarf."** Kids have reported the scarf stolen from the statue. It's a gentle, funny mystery that lets Mani and Preethi laugh together for once. Meet **Big Lou**, a trucker who runs Highway 17 every night and talks on CB radio. He warns them: "Two black SUVs been running up and down this road all week. No plates up front."
2. **Day 3, Marathon, Nipigon, Thunder Bay.** The Nipigon River Bridge. Superior's frozen edge. The Sleeping Giant on the horizon at sunset. A Tom Portons in Thunder Bay. Nila checks in, voice weaker.
3. **Night 3: Dr. Okafor's house, Thunder Bay.** Nila's former criminology professor, **Dr. Grace Okafor**, now teaches at the university there. Over dinner she and Nila (on the radio) build a profile of Hades: a collector of rare, dangerous things who needs control, and who will want to watch Mani arrive. "He won't kill you at a distance, Mani. He'll want you in the room." She gives them an old OPP contact name in Red Lake: Constable Rachel Dunn.
4. **Day 4, Highway 17 west, snowstorm near Ignace.** Whiteout. A jackknifed transport truck blocks the highway. It's a trap. **First gunfight of Part 2.** Kane's men come out of two black SUVs. The player learns both combat styles here:
  - **Stealth:** slip into the ditch, flank through the trees with Preethi on the shotgun, quiet takedowns.
  - **Aggressive:** M4 from behind the car, Desert Eagles, fight forward. Kane's voice on their radio: "Just the file, Mani. Nobody has to freeze tonight." They win. In the back of an SUV: **the first grenade case**, which goes into their trunk. Ivy's car is shot up but she's unhurt.
5. **Night 4: motel in Dryden.** The third motel. **Small mystery: "The Fuel Line."** In the morning the car won't start. Someone cut the fuel line. Clues: footprints in fresh snow from the office door, a night clerk with new boots and $200 in fresh bills, a Northstar business card in the trash. Mani confronts the clerk, who admits a man paid him. Choice: report him or flip him. Flipped, he tells them Northstar runs a supply convoy up Highway 105 every Thursday night.
6. **Day 5, Dryden to Vermilion Bay to Highway 105.** The road narrows. Forest on both sides for 170 km. Ear Falls: coffee at a roadside diner, the last stop before Red Lake. Nila, coughing, tells them how proud she is.
7. **Arrival, Red Lake.** Dusk, snow falling. The Norseman monument on the waterfront, white with frost. Headframes against the sky. They check into the **Northern Star Motel**; Bev Arsenault, the owner, eyes their luggage and the long cases. "Hunting?" "Something like that."
8. **Cliffhanger.** In their room, on the bed: a gift box with a silk ribbon. Inside, the shed skin of a golden lancehead, and a photo of Nila asleep in her hospital bed in Windsor, taken from inside her room. A card in neat handwriting:

*"Welcome to my town."*

## Episodes 3 to 6 · The investigation

### Episode 3 · Gold Country

The investigation begins. Mani and Preethi build the case against Northstar from Red Lake's hospital, museum and mineral collection, and meet the two locals who will matter most.

1. **Red Lake Margaret Cochenour Memorial Hospital.** Preethi poses as a visiting pharmacist. **Dr. Helen Marchetti** shows her four patient files: former Northstar contract workers, all with the same atypical cancer, all dead within months. The same strange blood marker as Nila. **Puzzle:** Preethi compares the five blood panels and finds the shared compound.
2. **Red Lake Regional Heritage Centre.** Mani studies the Hayden family: prospectors in the 1930s gold rush; the Hayden Deep Mine opened in 1946 and closed in 1991; R. Hayden left for engineering work in the south in the 1970s (the dam at Old Merrow, which Mani recognises); his son Mark came back in 2015 and bought the mine through Northstar.
3. **Dickenson Mine viewing lot, Balmertown.** A quiet moment over the mine landscape. Nila on the radio is weaker. Preethi and Mani talk about what happens if they fail.
4. **Erle Crull mineral collection, Balmertown Municipal Office.** **Puzzle:** a gold specimen labelled *"Hayden Deep, Level 6, 1962"* has a second, older tag underneath: *"Level 9"*. Public mine maps show only six levels. There is a hidden lower level.
5. **Constable Rachel Dunn, OPP.** She's been told about two strangers with long gun cases. A tense interview. Mani gives her the name from Dr. Okafor. She doesn't help, but she doesn't arrest them.
6. **Floatplane base: Daniel Keeper.** A bush pilot with a Norseman on skis. His cousin was one of Marchetti's four patients. He knows where the Burn is and agrees to fly them "when it's time".
7. **Cliffhanger.** Night radio check-in. Nila is reading the five blood panels back to them when her voice stops mid-word. A long tone. Then Amma's voice on the radio, in panic: *"Kanna, nurses vandhurukkaanga—"* (Kanna, the nurses are here—). Static.

### Episode 4 · The Ice Road

Nila survives the night. Mani and Preethi cross the ice road to McKenzie Island to find the one man who still remembers Level 9, and come back to find Ivy taken.

1. **Morning.** Nila is stable, but weaker. Her voice is a whisper on the radio. She has one instruction: "Find the man who dug it."
2. **Cochenour.** Asking around the bar and the general store leads to **Sully O'Rourke**, 78, on McKenzie Island.
3. **Set piece: the ice road at night.** The ferry is out for winter; the ice road is open but thin near the shore. Kane's snowmobiles chase them across the lake. Choices: drive the marked road or cut across black ice (faster, cracking). Preethi fires from the passenger window. The ice breaks behind them.
4. **Sully's cabin, McKenzie Island.** Whisky, a wood stove, stories. He dug Level 9 in 1974, "for something that wasn't gold". He has the only map of it, hand-drawn on the back of a calendar. He also says something no one follows up: "The old man never died, you know. They say he lives out there with the boy." *(Part 3 seed: R. Hayden.)*
5. **Puzzle: the map.** Match Sully's map to Ivy's surface map and the mineral-sample clue. Find a way in: the old ventilation shaft, from the Madsen side.
6. **Cliffhanger.** Back at the Northern Star Motel: Bev tied to a chair in the office, bruised and furious. Ivy's room is torn apart. A drop of blood on the snow outside. On the radio, Kane: "The journalist talks a lot. Thursday night, Mani. Bring the file, and you can have whatever's left of her."

### Episode 5 · Hayden Deep

Mani and Preethi ride the Thursday convoy into the mine, find the venom farm and the proof that Nila was poisoned on purpose, rescue Ivy, and learn the cure is kept somewhere else.

1. **Thursday night, Highway 105.** Using the Dryden clerk's tip, they hide in the last truck of Northstar's supply convoy. Through the gate, stealth.
2. **Surface buildings.** *Hitman*-style infiltration: steal a lab coat and badge, use Ivy's map, avoid cameras, or go loud. Grenade case #2 in a guard post.
3. **Down to Level 9.** The old ventilation shaft from Sully's map. A rusted ladder down 300 metres of darkness.
4. **The venom farm.** Hundreds of golden lanceheads in heated glass tanks. Robots milking venom. Freezers of peptide samples. It is beautiful and terrible.
5. **The HX-7 lab and the files.** Mani plugs the encrypted Hades file into the lab server: it is a key. It unlocks the test records.
  - **Subject GH-01: Nila.** Dosed via drinking water, Pike Point. Status: progressing as expected. Note in Hades's hand: *"Keep her alive until the brother comes."*
  - The four Red Lake workers, Subjects RL-01 to RL-04.
  - **The cure, HX-7C:** only three doses exist, all in "the house vault". Not here.
6. **Rescue Ivy.** She's held in a cold store. Bruised, alive, and furious. Preethi treats her.
7. **Set piece: escape.** Alarms. Kane's men in the tunnels. A mine cage elevator ride up under fire. Grenades bring down a tunnel behind them.
8. **Cliffhanger.** On the surface, at dawn, a ring of headlights: OPP cruisers. Constable Dunn, gun drawn: "Hands where I can see them. Both of you."

### Episode 6 · Starratt-Olsen

Dunn sees the evidence and quietly lets them go. Nila goes silent for a day and Amma takes the radio. Kane forces a showdown in a near-ghost town, and Daniel's plane is shot down over the Burn.

1. **OPP detachment.** Dunn reads the GH-01 file. Her face changes. She can't touch Northstar without a warrant, and Hades owns half the county. Off the record: "I didn't see you. Get your sister her medicine."
2. **Nila goes silent.** Nila has slipped into unconsciousness. For the whole episode, **Amma** is on the radio, with Appa beside her. Amma speaks Tanglish with fierce calm. Appa reads Nila's notes aloud to help them. The emotional low point.
3. **Kane's demand.** The file for Ivy's life is no longer enough: Kane wants Mani himself. Meeting at **Starratt-Olsen**, the near-ghost town, at midnight.
4. **Set piece: Starratt-Olsen.** An ambush in a blizzard among empty houses. The two characters split: Preethi takes a high position with the M4 in an old church bell tower; Mani moves house to house. Stealth or aggressive. Grenade case #3 in Kane's truck. Kane escapes, wounded.
5. **Nila wakes.** Her first words on the radio, barely audible: "Anna… Anni… I heard everything." She tells them where the mansion must be: the only heated building on a thermal map she asked Jonah to pull from a public satellite.
6. **Daniel's flight.** The Norseman takes off on skis at dawn, over the Burn. Below: endless forest, a frozen private lake, a stone-and-glass mansion, and behind it, a black cone of rock, steaming. **The Smoke.**
7. **Cliffhanger.** A rifle shot punches through the fuselage. Another hits the engine. Black smoke. Daniel fights the controls. The Norseman glides down toward a frozen lake in the heart of the Burn. Impact. White.

## Episode 7 · The Burn

Stranded in Hades's private forest, Mani and Preethi fight their way through Kane's hunters to the mansion. This is the combat episode: open forest areas, both characters fighting, every encounter playable with stealth or aggression, and Nila turning her radio into a weapon.

**How combat works in the Burn**

| Style | Tools | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stealth** | Disguises (hunters' white camouflage and radios), silent takedowns, snowfall to cover footprints, hiding in drifts and fallen timber | Mani takes a hunter's parka and radio and walks right through a checkpoint |
| **Distraction** | Throwing objects, flares, the drone, triggering snow to fall off branches, bait that draws wolves to an enemy camp | Preethi drops a flare in a dry thicket; the patrol runs to it |
| **Radio tricks (Nila)** | Nila listens to Kane's channel and broadcasts fake orders in Kane's style: "All units, regroup at the lookout." | Clears a path for Mani, and later lures half the hunters onto thin ice |
| **Environmental** | Thin lake ice, frozen deadfall, avalanche slopes, gas cans at camps, wolves | A grenade on a snow cornice buries a patrol |
| **Aggressive** | M4, shotgun, Desert Eagles, grenades; cover behind rocks and fallen trees | Shotgun clearing a hunting cabin room by room |

If detected in stealth, the encounter becomes a firefight; it never fails outright. Each area rewards a clean stealth run with extra ammo and a short line from Nila.

**The episode**

1. **The crash.** The Norseman lies on its skis on a frozen lake, one wing cracked. Daniel's leg is broken. Preethi splints it with paracord and a seat brace. The radio survives. Daniel stays with the plane and keeps a flare gun; he will try to repair the radio for the extraction. "Go. I'll be here when you come back. Probably."
2. **Area 1, the frozen lake shore.** Hunters on snowmobiles sweep the lake. The tutorial area for disguises: take the first hunter's whites and radio.
3. **Area 2, the burned ridge.** A forest of black, dead trunks above new pines. A sniper on the ridge. Preethi's long-range duel with the M4; Mani spots for her with the binoculars. Grenade case #4 in the sniper's nest.
4. **Area 3, split paths.** The ridge splits. The player plays both halves, switching between characters.
  - **Mani, the north trail:** a hunters' camp with dogs. Stealth through the tents, or bait the wolves in.
  - **Preethi, the ravine:** a frozen creek and an old trapper's cabin used as an ammo dump. Shotgun-close fighting, or slip past. They reunite at an old fire lookout tower.
5. **Area 4, the thin ice.** Nila, barely strong enough, broadcasts on Kane's channel in his own clipped style: "All units, intercept on the south lake." Half the hunters drive their snowmobiles onto ice that can't hold them. *(Optional choice: warn them on the radio and save them, or let it happen. Nila asks Mani which.)*
6. **Boss fight: Kane at the fire lookout.** Kane, wounded from Starratt-Olsen, waits at the lookout tower with a heavy rifle. A three-phase fight:
  - **Phase 1:** Kane snipes from the tower while Mani and Preethi close in from two sides.
  - **Phase 2:** inside the tower stairwell, close quarters.
  - **Phase 3:** on the open platform in the wind. Kane holds Preethi over the rail. Mani has one grenade and one magazine. Kane falls from the tower. Before he dies, Kane says: "He'll give you the cure. He wants you to watch which one he gives her."
7. **Cliffhanger.** From the lookout platform, at dusk: the frozen private lake below, and across it the **Hades Mansion**, every window lit. Behind it, the Smoke breathes a column of steam into the cold. Something large and striped paces behind a glass wall in the courtyard. The radio clicks onto their channel. Hades, warm, pleased:

## Episode 8 · The Hades Mansion

Mani and Preethi break into the mansion through its animal enclosures, face Hades for the first time, choose the right vial of the cure under his eyes, and escape as the Smoke erupts. Nila lives. Part 2 ends at Pongal, with a hook into Part 3.

**The mansion.** A stone-and-glass house on a frozen private lake, heated by pipes that run from the Smoke behind it: Hades taps the volcanic heat to keep his animals alive in a land of −35°C. Each wing is a level with its own danger. The player can explore in any order, with stealth or force.

| Area | Danger | Way through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gatehouse and kennels** | Wolf-dog hybrids, two guards | Bear spray, distraction, or a quiet entry over the boathouse roof |

| **Hades's study and the vault** | Hades | The finale |

**The finale**

1. **Hades, face to face.** The study: firelight, tea in a china pot, a golden lancehead coiled on the desk like a cat. **Mark Hayden**, 58, silver-haired, soft-spoken, in the long coat. For the first time the player sees his face. He talks like an old friend: Greyhaven, Victor ("He talked too much, so I stopped him"), his father, Nila. "Your sister is the most interesting person I've ever listened to."
2. **The offer.** On the desk: three identical vials. One is HX-7C, the cure. Two are HX-7, the poison. "Give me the file, and choose. I'll even let you take the one you pick. I want to see if you can save her."
3. **Puzzle: the vials.** Preethi's moment. Using her pharmacy kit and what she learned at the Hayden Deep lab (the shared compound from Episode 3, the HX-7C formula from the files), she runs three quick tests: colour reaction, crystal pattern under the drone's camera lens, and a pH strip. One vial passes. Hades watches, delighted. "Oh, she's good."
4. **The fight.** Mani hands over the USB, which Nila has wiped clean over the radio. Hades realises, and smiles. Then the vents beneath the house roar: Daniel, back at the plane, has fired a flare to signal Dunn, and the OPP helicopter is coming; Hades triggers the house's emergency vents to cover his escape. Steam, falling glass, loose animals. A running fight through the burning conservatory and out onto the slope of the Smoke.
5. **Hades's fall.** On the black rock above the vents, Hades, the golden lancehead on his arm, faces Mani one last time. The ground cracks. A column of scalding steam erupts between them. When it clears, Hades is gone, fallen into the fissure. No body is found.
6. **Extraction.** An OPP helicopter lands on the frozen lake: Constable Dunn, and Daniel strapped to a stretcher, grinning. Ivy has sent the lab files to every newspaper in the country.
7. **Windsor.** The hospital. Preethi hands the vial to Nila's oncologist with a page of notes. The first dose. A long wait. Nila's eyes open. Tanglish: "Anna… Anni… neenga romba late." (You two are very late.)
8. **Pongal, January.** Appa said it in Part 1: "Come home for Pongal." The family celebrates in Mani and Preethi's apartment: the pongal pot boiling over for luck, kolam at the door, sugarcane, Amma and Appa in person this time. Nila, thinner but laughing, back on her feet.

## Open decisions

- **Where the guns come from.** In Canada an M4 is prohibited, a Desert Eagle is restricted, and grenades are illegal. Options: a black-market dealer in Windsor reached through a Part 1 contact; Hades's own shipment recovered at the Greyhaven mill in Ep 1; or no explanation, as action fiction.
- **The Smoke.** An ancient volcano that impossibly wakes, or a decades-old mine fire that locals call a volcano? The first suits a Part 3 mystery; the second is more realistic.
- **The Part 3 hook.** Keep the pregnancy and R. Hayden reveal, or choose another?
- **Preethi's background.** Pharmacist and former air-rifle shooter, as written, or something else?
- **Episode 7, the thin-ice choice.** Keep the moral choice of saving or drowning the hunters?
