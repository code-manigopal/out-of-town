# Out of Town: Greyhaven

Oct 20, 2026 · @Mani

## Overview

Part 1 of the *Out of Town* series: Mani drives from Windsor to the lake town of Greyhaven for Diwali with his sister Nila and finds her gone, and the town hiding a drug lab and an exotic-animal smuggling ring behind a closed paper mill.

**Premise.** Mani (29) and Nila (25) are Indian immigrants from Vellore, Tamil Nadu. Their parents still live in India. Mani lives in Windsor. Nila studied criminology, but she always wanted to be on the radio. A year ago she took a job as the late-night radio host of *After Hours* on Lake Merrow 94.1 FM in Greyhaven, Ontario, and moved into a shared flat above a motel. With Diwali coming and their parents 12,000 km away, she invites Mani for the weekend. On their last call she says she needs to show him something, "not on the phone". Then she stops answering.

**The truth behind Greyhaven**

- **The front.** Merrow Paper Mill closed in 2009. It never really stopped running.
- **The drugs.** Victor Kessler runs a fentanyl pill press in the mill's old pulping hall. The lab's chemical waste goes into blue barrels, and the barrels go into Lake Merrow, on top of the village of Old Merrow, drowned by a dam in 1978. The lake is slowly poisoning the town.
- **The animals.** In the mill's heated boiler house, Victor keeps smuggled exotic animals sold to rich private collectors: poison dart frogs, slow lorises, rare tortoises, and his prize stock, golden lancehead vipers from Ilha da Queimada Grande ("Snake Island") off Brazil, one of the rarest and most venomous snakes in the world. Their venom is also sold. Animals and pills move on the same night boats across Lake Erie and the same trucks.
- **The law.** Sheriff Dale Corbin takes a monthly retainer to look the other way.
- **The partner.** Victor is not the top. He answers to a partner known only as **Hades**. His real name, **Mark Hayden**, appears a few times in Part 1 on paper and in passing, but nobody connects the two. Hades is also the figure in the long coat who watches from a distance in every episode. He is the villain of Part 2.
- **Nila's discovery.** Callers to her show kept describing sick pets, rashes, strange trucks at night and, once, a snake nobody could identify on a dock. Her criminology training kicked in: she logged every call, mapped every report, and saw the pattern. She went off-script on air. The mill is the station's biggest sponsor, so her show was pulled. She kept investigating, and broadcasting what she found on her own ham radio at 7.185 MHz. Then someone came for her.

**Timeline.** The story runs from Tuesday to Sunday in early November, ending on Diwali night.

**How episodes play.** Each episode runs 25 to 30 minutes in the browser version and about 40 minutes in a full 3D version. That gives 3.5 to 4 hours for the whole of Part 1 in the browser version. Every episode has walking and talking, an investigation, a puzzle, one tense set piece (stealth, a chase, a gunfight, a dive) and a cliffhanger. Mani is not a fighter. He is a software engineer with a data analytics degree, and many puzzles use that: logs, patterns, records. He only gets a gun in Episode 5, and he never gets comfortable with it.

**Language and subtitles.** Mani, Nila, Amma and Appa speak to each other in Tanglish, the everyday mix of Tamil and English that Tamil families use. Everyone else speaks English. Every line of dialogue in the game shows an English subtitle, including lines already in English, so subtitles stay on screen consistently. In this script, Tanglish lines are followed by their English subtitle in brackets. Tamil words of address used throughout: **Anna** (elder brother), **Kanna** (dear, used by Amma), **Amma** (mother), **Appa** (father).

**Fictional names.** Mani's former employer is called Auragle, and the family business is Sri Valli Leathers in Ranipet.

This screenplay follows the one true ending. Choices and alternative endings will be added afterwards.

## Episode 1 · County Roads

Mani drives from Windsor to Greyhaven, solves two small mysteries at roadside coffee shops, meets a friendly stranger named Victor, and arrives to find Nila gone.

**SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN. BLACK SCREEN.**

Radio static. A phone voicemail plays, timestamped *Sunday 23:52*.

**NILA (V.O.):** Anna, naan dhaan. Station-ku call pannadha, okay? Plan panna maadhiri Tuesday vandhudu. Naan oru vishayam kandupidichiruken. Phone-la solla mudiyaadhu. Inga vandhappo edhavadhu weird-aa irundha, Ruth kitta mattum pesu, vera yaar kittayum illa. Anna, yaara venaalum nambu, aana andha— (Anna, it's me. Don't call the station, okay? Just come Tuesday like we planned. I found something. I can't say it on the phone. If anything feels weird when you get here, talk only to Ruth, nobody else. Anna, trust anyone, but don't trust the—)

The message cuts. Silence.

**TITLE CARD:** OUT OF TOWN: GREYHAVEN. EPISODE 1. COUNTY ROADS.

**SCENE 2 — INT. MANI'S APARTMENT, WINDSOR — TUESDAY, 09:05**

A small one-bedroom. A whiteboard of job applications, half of them crossed out. A graduation photo from the University of Windsor. On the counter: a box of homemade-style laddu and mysore pak, a packet of clay diyas, and a gift-wrapped handheld ham radio with a bow.

Mani, 29, packs a duffel. He plays Nila's voicemail again. Calls her. Straight to voicemail.

**\[PLAY\]** Walk around the apartment. Pack three items into the duffel: the sweets, the diyas, the gift radio. Optional: look at the whiteboard (Mani's notes: "Data Analytics Engineer, 2nd round Thurs"), the graduation photo, and a framed photo of Nila at her first broadcast.

His laptop rings. A video call from Vellore. At 9:30 in the morning: Amma and Appa, 7 pm their time, Appa still in his shop shirt.

**AMMA:** Kanna, saaptiya? Moonji ellam ilachu pochu. (Kanna, did you eat? Your face has gone so thin.)

**MANI:** Saapten, Amma. (I ate, Amma.)

**APPA:** Company-la irundhu edhavadhu news? Andha Thursday interview? (Any news from the company? That Thursday interview?)

**MANI:** Second round, Appa. Thursday dhaan. (Second round, Appa. On Thursday.)

**AMMA:** Nila enga? Moonu naala phone edukkala. En mela kovama irukkaala? (And Nila? Three days she hasn't picked up. Is she angry with me?)

**\[PLAY\]** Choice: *"Avalukku work busy, Amma" (She's busy with work, Amma)* (reassure) or *"Inniki avala paakka poren, ippo dhaan kilambaren" (I'm going to see her today, I'm leaving now)* (half-truth). Both continue. This sets a small flag: how honest Mani is with his parents, which pays off in Episode 8.

**APPA:** Sweets eduthutu po. Kadai nalla poguthu, kavala padaadhenu Appa sonnaarnu sollu. Pongal-ku veetuku vara sollu. (Take her the sweets. Tell her Appa says the shop is doing fine, she should stop worrying. Tell her to come home for Pongal.)

**AMMA** (leaning into frame before he can hang up): Innoru vishayam, Kanna. Preethi-nu oru ponnu, Windsor-la dhaan irukkaa. Pharmacist. Photo anupparen, paaru. (One more thing, Kanna. There's a girl called Preethi, she lives in Windsor too. A pharmacist. I'm sending her photo, have a look.)

**MANI:** Amma! Ippo venaam! (Amma! Not now!)

*(Part 2 seed: Preethi becomes Mani's wife.)*

Mani closes the laptop. Stares at the gift radio.

**SCENE 3 — EXT. COUNTY ROAD 20, ESSEX COUNTY — MORNING**

Mani's grey sedan heads east along Lake Erie. Greenhouses glitter outside Leamington. Flat farmland, grain silos, a hawk on a fence post.

**\[PLAY\]** Driving segment. Steer, use the car radio, and read texts at stops. Mani's phone mounts on the dash. He sends a text to Nila's flatmate, Tess: *"Hi Tess, it's Mani, Nila's brother. Is she around? Coming up today."* No reply yet.

**SCENE 4 — INT. TOM PORTONS, COUNTY ROAD 20 — 10:55**

A busy coffee-and-donut shop. Red-and-cream decor, a hockey game rerun on the TV. Mani orders a coffee and an apple fritter.

At the counter, an old man, **HAROLD**, 81, flat cap, slaps his receipt down.

**HAROLD:** Third day running! I order a double-double and somebody's giving me decaf! I've been coming here since 1974.

The young cashier, **PRIYA**, looks helpless. Mani, who has nothing better to do while the coffee brews, steps in.

**\[PLAY\] Small mystery: "The Decaf Problem."** Investigate.

- **Clue 1:** Harold's three receipts. Each order was rung up correctly as regular.
- **Clue 2:** The drive-thru log on the screen behind the counter. A second order for "Harold" was placed by phone app every morning, two minutes before he arrives, marked decaf.
- **Clue 3:** The app account's profile picture is a cat. Harold's table has a phone case with the same cat.
- **Clue 4:** Harold's wife, **JUNE**, sitting by the window, very interested in her newspaper.

**\[PLAY\]** Confront the right person. Pointing at June solves it.

**JUNE:** The doctor said no caffeine after his heart thing, and he's a stubborn old goat!

**HAROLD:** June! Fifty-two years and you're sabotaging my coffee?

They bicker, then laugh. Priya gives Mani his fritter for free.

**PRIYA:** You should be a detective.

**MANI:** I'm a data guy. It's just logs.

*(This teaches the core loop: examine, collect clues in the journal, then accuse or choose.)*

**SCENE 5 — EXT. COUNTY ROAD, NORTH OF CHATHAM — MIDDAY**

Farmland turns to forest. The sky goes grey. Mani's phone buzzes. Tess replies.

**TESS (TEXT):** *haven't seen her since sunday night. thought she was with u?? her phone is here at the flat*

Mani pulls over on the gravel shoulder. Calls Nila again. Voicemail. He grips the wheel, then drives on faster.

**SCENE 6 — INT. TOM PORTONS, HIGHWAY 21 TRUCK STOP — 14:20**

A quieter rural Tom Portons beside a gas station. Rain on the windows. A refrigerated box truck marked *MERROW PALLETS & SUPPLY* is parked outside.

Inside, a burly man in a diner apron under his jacket, **GUS PELL**, 55, is arguing with a teenage employee.

**GUS:** I left the cooler on the seat for two minutes. Two minutes!

A well-dressed man at the window table, **VICTOR KESSLER**, 47, tailored wool coat, silver watch, sips black coffee and watches with mild amusement.

**\[PLAY\] Small mystery: "The Missing Cooler."** Gus's blue cooler vanished from his unlocked truck cab.

- **Clue 1:** Wet sneaker prints from the truck to the back of the restaurant.
- **Clue 2:** The staff room door is ajar. Two teens on break, giggling.
- **Clue 3:** One teen's backpack is soaking wet and bulges.

As Mani works it out, Victor gets up and joins him, as if it's a game.

**VICTOR:** Kids think every cooler is full of beer. Shall we?

Together they find the cooler in the staff room. Victor gets to it first and opens the lid just an inch. Mani glimpses a heat pack and a ventilated plastic box with something coiled inside, before Victor shuts it.

**VICTOR:** Bait. Gus fishes. Don't you, Gus?

**GUS** (taking it, not meeting Mani's eye): Yeah. Bait.

Gus leaves fast. Victor turns to Mani, all warmth.

**VICTOR:** Sharp eyes. What do you do?

**MANI:** Software. Data. Between jobs.

**VICTOR:** Ah, the most dangerous kind of person. Someone who notices patterns. Where are you headed?

**\[PLAY\]** Dialogue choice: tell him the truth ("Greyhaven, visiting my sister for Diwali"), or keep it vague ("North, visiting family"). Either way, Victor notices the sweets box on the passenger seat through the window.

**VICTOR:** Diwali. Festival of lights. Good triumphs over evil, isn't it? I've always liked that story. (beat) Greyhaven's a strange little town. Fog, a lake, a priest who rings a bell for nobody. Lovely.

His phone buzzes. He glances at it.

**VICTOR** (into phone, turning away): Mark. Yes, I'm on the road. Tonight. It'll be clean.

He hangs up, drops a twenty on the counter, and pays for Mani's coffee. He slides a card across: *KESSLER RESTRUCTURING — Victor Kessler, Principal.*

**VICTOR:** If you ever want work, I hire people who notice things. Drive safe, Mani. The county roads get dark early this time of year.

Mani realises later that he never told Victor his name. It was on his coffee cup.

**HADES SIGHTING:** At the far gas pump, a black car with tinted windows. A man in a long coat stands beside it, not pumping gas, facing the restaurant. When Mani walks out, the car is gone.

**SCENE 7 — EXT. GREYHAVEN, MAIN STREET — 17:40, DUSK**

Fog sits on Lake Merrow like something waiting. Three streetlights, sodium orange. A shuttered hardware store. The Merrow Diner, lit and almost empty. St. Brendan's church bell rings six times, though there is no service.

A bronze plaque at the lakeside: *OLD MERROW, 1842–1978. A village given to the water so the valley could prosper.*

**\[PLAY\]** Free walk down Main Street. Read the plaque. Look through the diner window (Tess, in an apron, sees Mani and waves him to wait). Look at the radio station, a small brick building with a dark 94.1 sign.

**SCENE 8 — INT. LAKEVIEW MOTEL OFFICE — 18:00**

The Lakeview's neon sign has two dead letters. Behind the desk, **RUTH VANE**, 64, reading glasses on a chain, a police scanner murmuring, a shotgun leaning within reach.

**RUTH** (not looking up): Rooms are forty a night.

**MANI:** I'm Nila's brother. Mani. She lives upstairs.

Ruth looks up slowly. Studies his face like she's checking it against one she knows.

**\[PLAY\]** How Mani handles Ruth sets her trust, which matters all game.

- *"She told me to talk to you and nobody else."* (truth, Ruth trust +2)
- *"I'm just here for Diwali, is she around?"* (casual, +1)
- *"Has the sheriff been asking about her?"* (too direct, Ruth goes guarded, +0)

**RUTH** (with trust): You've got her eyes. (beat) She hasn't been home since Sunday. Her car's in the lot. I called Corbin Monday. He said she's a grown woman and probably ran off with somebody. (She slides a key across.) Flat's up the back stairs. Tess has the other key. And Mani—I sleep light, and I keep this on all night. (She taps the scanner.)

**SCENE 9 — INT. NILA'S FLAT — 18:30**

A small two-bedroom above the motel. Fairy lights half-strung along the window. A rangoli of coloured chalk on the floor by the door, half-finished, a footprint smudged through it. Posters of old radio stations. A cork board covered in caller notes.

**TESS MARSH**, 19, comes in breathless, still in her diner apron.

**TESS:** You're Mani. She talks about you like, constantly. "Anna would know what to do." (beat) She left Sunday night with her radio bag. She said she'd be back by two. She wasn't.

**\[PLAY\]** Light investigation (the deep search is in Episode 2).

- Nila's phone on the kitchen table, dead. Mani charges it. It is locked.
- The cork board: sticky notes from listeners. *"My dog drank lake water and died." "Trucks on the mill road at 2am again." "Rash on my kid's arms, doctor says eczema." "Saw a gold snake on Marsh dock??"*
- A printout pinned in the centre: *AFTER HOURS — SUSPENDED UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE. Brian Holt, Station Manager.*
- The half-finished rangoli, with a muddy boot print through it. Size 11.

**TESS:** They pulled her show two weeks ago. Brian said "sponsor complaints". The mill's the sponsor. (beat) There's a mill? It's been closed my whole life.

Tess has to go back for the end of her shift. She hesitates at the door.

**TESS:** Lock this. Please.

**SCENE 10 — INT. MANI'S CAR, MOTEL LOT — 22:58. CLIFFHANGER.**

Mani sits in his car to warm up, the gift radio in its wrapping on the passenger seat. Out of habit he tunes the car radio to 94.1, Nila's station. Dead air. Faint hiss.

The dashboard clock turns 23:00.

The hiss shifts. A voice, live, close, tired.

**NILA (ON RADIO):** This is *After Hours*. If anyone's still listening—(static)—Anna, parking-la irukkiradhu nee dhaana? Ennai thedi varaadha. Veetuku po. Please. (Anna, is that you in the lot? Don't come looking for me. Go home. Please.)

Mani grabs the radio dial.

**MANI:** Nila? NILA!

Silence. Then a second voice on the same frequency. A man. Calm, unhurried, almost kind.

**MAN'S VOICE (ON RADIO):** He's already here, Nila.

The signal dies. In the fog across the lake, far out on the water, a single red light blinks twice, then goes dark.

**END OF EPISODE 1.**

**What carries forward:** Ruth's trust. How honest Mani was with his parents. Victor's card in Mani's journal. The boot print photographed.

## Episode 2 · Dead Air

Mani searches Nila's flat through the night, hides from the sheriff, hears the tape of Nila's last show, and learns that the man who bought her silence is Victor Kessler.

**SCENE 1 — EXT. LAKEVIEW MOTEL LOT — TUESDAY, 23:02**

Recap of the broadcast. Mani stumbles out of the car to the edge of the lot and shouts Nila's name across the fogged lake. Nothing. Ruth's office light snaps on. She comes out in a cardigan, scanner in hand.

**RUTH:** I heard it. Both voices.

**MANI:** Where did it come from?

**RUTH:** Somewhere on the lake. Could be a boat, could be anywhere. (beat) That man's voice. I've heard it before, on my scanner, late. Never says a name. Just listens, and every so often, he answers.

**SCENE 2 — INT. NILA'S FLAT — 23:20**

Mani can't sleep. He decides to search properly.

**\[PLAY\] Investigation: the flat.** Five clues, any order.

- **Under the mattress:** a page torn from Nila's radio log. Columns of dates, times, frequencies. Every night since October 21: *23:00, 7.185 MHz, "PP"*. The last line underlined twice: *"If I'm not here, I'm there."*
- **The window:** her antenna cable hangs outside, cut clean. Mani coils it up and keeps it. *(This cable is needed in Episode 7.)*
- **The bathroom:** Mani runs the hot tap. As the mirror steams, letters appear where a finger once traced them: **MILL**.
- **The desk:** Nila's laptop, locked. A sticky note on the lid in her handwriting: *"Appa's shop + the year Anna joined Auragle."*
- **Under the bed:** her radio bag is gone. Its empty spot in the dust, and a single gold-and-brown snake scale, the size of a fingernail, caught in the carpet.

**\[PLAY\] Puzzle: the laptop password.** Mani knows the answer if the player does: the family shop is Sri Valli Leathers; Mani joined Auragle in 2018. Password: **SriValli2018**. Two wrong tries give a hint from Mani's memory.

Inside the laptop:

- **A spreadsheet**, *"Callers.xlsx"*: 46 listener reports, each with a date and a location. Mani the analyst plots them on the town map. Almost every one sits along the lakeshore or on the mill road.
- **Audio folder:** call recordings from *After Hours*. The last one is dated October 20 and won't play. *"File stored at station."*
- **An unsent email draft** to a Toronto journalist: *"I have callers, photos and a dive planned. Not just dumping. Something alive is moving through this town."*
- **A photo** from the Marsh family dock: a thick gold snake in the reeds, blurry.

**MANI** (to himself): Something alive…

**SCENE 3 — INT. NILA'S FLAT, HALLWAY — 01:15**

Floorboards creak outside. A flashlight beam slides under the door and stops. A key scrapes in the lock.

**MAN (O.S.):** Ruth said nobody's up here.

**\[PLAY\] Timed choice (8 seconds):**

- **Hide in the closet** (best). Through the slats, Mani watches a heavy man in a sheriff's jacket, **SHERIFF DALE CORBIN**, step inside. He doesn't search. He looks at the window, at the missing cable, and at the open laptop, which Mani has just closed. He touches its warm lid. Pause. "Stubborn family," he says, and leaves.
- **Climb out the window.** Mani drops into the wet grass and hears the door open above. Danger +1.
- **Open the door.** Corbin fills the doorway and smiles like a man who has already decided what Mani is. Danger +2. He writes Mani's licence plate in a notebook.

**CORBIN** (if seen): Long way from Windsor. Your sister's an adult. Adults leave. I'd start driving early, before the fog.

**SCENE 4 — INT. MERROW DINER — WEDNESDAY, 08:30**

Vinyl booths, a pie case, a bulletin board of lost-pet posters (far too many). Locals stop talking when Mani walks in. Tess pours his coffee and slips him a refill of jam without being asked.

Gus comes out of the kitchen, wiping his hands, and stops dead when he sees Mani.

**GUS** (forced smile): Truck stop guy. Small world. Breakfast is on me.

**\[PLAY\]** Talk to three locals at the counter (optional, adds atmosphere and clues):

- **MRS. ABERNATHY**, retired teacher: her dog died in September after drinking from the lake.
- **CARL**, hunter: has seen a black car parked at the water tower at night. "Never see anyone get out." *(Hades.)*
- **A trucker:** "Mill road's busier now it's closed than it ever was open."

**\[PLAY\]** Choice with Gus: mention the cooler (Gus spills coffee and goes pale; Gus becomes nervous around Mani, danger +1) or say nothing (Gus relaxes).

**SCENE 5 — INT. LAKE MERROW 94.1 FM — 10:00**

A small brick station. Gold records from the 80s on the wall. A brass plaque at reception: *STUDIO RENEWAL 2024 MADE POSSIBLE BY THE HAYDEN FAMILY FOUNDATION.*

**BRIAN HOLT**, 44, station manager, polo shirt, too much cologne, meets Mani in the lobby and tries to keep him there.

**BRIAN:** Look, I'm sorry about Nila, I am. But her show was suspended for content reasons. She was making accusations on air.

**MANI:** Accusations against who?

**BRIAN:** Our sponsor. Merrow Mill Holdings keeps this station alive.

**\[PLAY\] Dialogue duel.** Get Brian to let Mani into the tape archive. Winning lines use what Mani found: the 46 caller reports, the cut antenna, the sheriff in her flat. Three good answers win. (If the player fails, Tess later sneaks Mani in through the back door, so the story continues either way.)

**SCENE 6 — INT. 94.1 FM, TAPE ARCHIVE — 10:20**

A cramped room of shelves, labelled by date.

**\[PLAY\] Puzzle: find the tape.** The archive is filed by a code (year, week, show number). Using Nila's radio log dates, work out the label for October 20: *AH-26-43-112*. Load it in the deck.

The tape plays. Nila's voice, warm and quick, then a caller, an old man, whispering.

**CALLER (WALTER, ON TAPE):** …I won't give my name. My son fishes. In seventy-seven I loaded barrels for the mill, down where the village was, before they flooded it. I was nineteen. And they're doing it again, miss. Every week. New barrels, new trucks. And the other thing… the animals.

**NILA (ON TAPE):** Animals?

**CALLER:** In crates. Some of 'em alive. I hear them at night from the dock. Hissing.

**NILA (ON TAPE):** Listeners, I'm going to the mill myself, and I'll tell you everything I find. Right here, right—

The tape cuts to a station ID jingle. Brian, in the doorway, rubs his face.

**BRIAN:** I cut the feed. Five minutes later I got a phone call. Not from the mill. From a consultant who'd just signed a two-year sponsorship. Paid in advance. Kessler. Victor Kessler.

Mani pulls Victor's business card from his wallet. Stares at it.

**SCENE 7 — EXT. 94.1 FM, PARKING LOT — 11:00**

Sheriff Corbin's cruiser is parked across the back of Mani's car. Corbin leans on it, eating a donut from a Tom Portons bag.

**CORBIN:** Filing a missing persons report? Good. Come by the station tomorrow and we'll do it properly. (beat) Today I'd stay out of folks' business. Folks here are private.

**\[PLAY\]** Dialogue: Mani can mention Victor (Corbin's jaw tightens, danger +1), mention Brian's tape (Corbin goes very quiet, danger +1), or stay polite (no change, Corbin trust +1, which unlocks an easier drawer moment in Episode 5).

**SCENE 8 — INT. NILA'S FLAT — 15:30. CLIFFHANGER.**

Mani plugs Nila's phone into the laptop. Same password family: it unlocks with **SriValli2001**, Nila's birth year.

The last voice memo, recorded Sunday 23:40, in the dark. Wind. Water.

**NILA (VOICE MEMO):** Anna, idha nee kettuttu irundha, naan unkitta nerla solla mudiyala. Eli-ku theriyum. Dock pathi Eli kitta kelu, avan mela kovapadaadha. Tess-ku bayandhu dhaan avan ipdi pannaan. (Anna, if you're hearing this, I couldn't tell you in person. Eli knows. Ask Eli about the dock, and don't be angry with him. He did it because he's scared for Tess.) (whispering) Andha coat potta aal thirumba vandhuttaan. Naan broadcast panra ovvoru raathiriyum avan inga irukkaan. Pakkathula varradhilla.
