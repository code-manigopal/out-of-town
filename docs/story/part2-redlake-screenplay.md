## Episode 1 · Lighthouse

Nila collapses on air and is diagnosed with a cancer Hades gave her. A night raid on the Greyhaven mill ruins points to Red Lake. Nila plans everything, including an illegal gun buy under the Ambassador Bridge, and Mani and Preethi start the drive north.

**SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN. INT. WINDSOR RADIO STUDIO — 23:40**

A small studio, red ON AIR light. **NILA**, 26, headphones on, her new late show.

**NILA (ON AIR):** …and that's why you never trust a man who says he "doesn't really like music". Next up, something slow for the night shift—

She coughs. Covers her mouth. Looks at her palm: blood. She keeps her voice perfectly steady.

**NILA (ON AIR):** —here it is.

She presses play. The song starts. She slides off her chair onto the floor. The ON AIR light stays red.

**TITLE CARD:** OUT OF TOWN: RED LAKE. EPISODE 1. LIGHTHOUSE.

**SCENE 2 — INT. HOSPITAL, WINDSOR — OVER FIVE DAYS**

A montage. Mani, 30, and **PREETHI**, 27, running through emergency doors. Scans. Waiting rooms. Preethi in her hospital pharmacy uniform, between shifts. An oncologist, **DR. SINGH**, with a tablet.

**DR. SINGH:** It's aggressive. And it's atypical. Honestly, I've never seen a progression like this. We'll start treatment, but I won't pretend I understand it.

Preethi reads the blood panel over his shoulder. She stops on one line.

**PREETHI:** What's this compound? It's not on any standard panel.

**DR. SINGH:** The lab flagged it as an artefact.

**PREETHI** (quietly, to Mani): It's not an artefact.

Amma and Appa arrive from Chennai, still in travel clothes. Amma takes Nila's hand.

**NILA** (weak smile): Onnum illa, Amma. Konjam blood, avlo dhaan. (It's nothing, Amma. Just a little blood, that's all.)

**APPA** (to Mani, in the corridor): Unmaiya sollu. Evlo serious? (Tell me the truth. How serious?)

**MANI:** Romba serious, Appa. (Very serious, Appa.)

**MANI:** So something only you had.

**SCENE 6 — INT. NILA'S HOSPITAL ROOM — NEXT DAY**

The label and note on Nila's bed tray. Nila looks stronger, the way people do when they have a job.

**NILA:** Red Lake. Northwestern Ontario. Gold country. End of the road. (She taps the label.) Northstar Biologics, registered 2015. Director: M. Hayden. The same Hayden from the plaque at the station. Victor's "Mark". (beat) He's not hiding, Anna. He's waiting.

**PREETHI:** Then we take it to the police.

**NILA:** With what? A label from a burned building. Ortiz already tried; the OPP said it's a Greyhaven case and Greyhaven's closed. (beat) Hades has the cure. I'd bet my life on it. I am betting my life on it.

**MANI:** Then we go.

**NILA:** Then you go prepared. Kane's people shot at you in Greyhaven. Up there it'll be worse. (beat) Which brings us to the guns.

Preethi looks between them.

**PREETHI:** We can buy hunting rifles, right? People up north have them.

**NILA:** Legally? A licence takes months: a course, a test, background checks. An M4-style rifle is prohibited in Canada outright. A Desert Eagle is a restricted handgun; you'd need a special licence and a permit just to drive it anywhere. Grenades, forget it. (beat) We don't have months, Anni. I have weeks.

**MANI:** So what are you saying?

**NILA:** I studied criminals for four years. Tonight I need one. (She opens her laptop.) Rocco Delisle. Auto body shop off Wyandotte West. Charged twice, never convicted. Brings guns over the river from Detroit. I covered his second trial for a class project. I know what he sells, I know what he charges, and I know he's scared of going back to court.

**PREETHI** (quietly): Nila, this is a crime.

**NILA:** So is what's happening in my blood. (beat) Anni, I'll understand if you don't go.

**\[PLAY\] Key choice for Preethi:** *"Naan varen. Ava en thangachi maadhiri."* (I'm coming. She's like my own sister.) / *"I'm coming, but we use them only to survive."* Both continue; the second sets the "restraint" flag, which affects a moment in Episode 7.

Nila is on the radio in Mani's ear, from her hospital bed.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** Let him talk first. He likes the sound of his own voice.

**ROCCO:** The radio girl's brother. She sent me a very polite message. Mentioned my court dates. Very polite. (He opens a case.) Pump shotgun. Twelve-gauge. M4, clean, no serial. Two Desert Eagles. Ammo. Forty grand.

**LIGHTHOUSE:** He charged the last buyer twenty-two. Say twenty-five.

**\[PLAY\] Haggling and inspection.** Three steps:

1. **Haggle.** Use Nila's lines to get the price down. Winning sets the price at $26,000.
2. **Inspect.** Preethi checks each weapon. One Desert Eagle has a filed-down firing pin; it won't fire. Spot it (look at the pin, compare it to the other gun).
3. **Confront.** *"This one's dead, Rocco."* He laughs and swaps it. "Your sister said you were sharp."

A police cruiser rolls along the riverfront road above.

**LIGHTHOUSE:** Patrol car, westbound, ninety seconds. Close the doors.

**\[PLAY\] Timed choice (10 seconds):** close the van and stand like you're chatting (correct), or run for the car (the cruiser slows; Rocco's men scatter; danger +1, and the deal finishes in a hurry with less ammo).

**ROCCO:** No grenades. I'm not crazy. (He pockets the cash.) And tell your sister, if she ever mentions my court dates again, I'll stop being polite.

## Episode 2 · Highway 17

The second half of the drive: Winnie-the-Pooh's hometown, a night with Nila's old professor, an ambush in a snowstorm, a sabotaged fuel line, and a welcome gift in Red Lake.

**SCENE 1 — INT. CAR, HIGHWAY 17 — DAY 3, MORNING**

Wawa behind them. Ivy's dented hatchback ahead. Snow on the shoulders. Preethi has the Hades recording on repeat on her phone.

**PREETHI:** "GH-01 is doing very well." He talks about her like a houseplant.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** That's what he wants you to feel. Angry people make mistakes. (cough) Don't give him that.

**MANI:** Nila, how are you feeling?

**LIGHTHOUSE:** Amma made me eat idli at six a.m. I'm feeling attacked. (beat) Drive, Anna.

**SCENE 2 — EXT. WHITE RIVER — DAY 3, 10:00**

A tiny town. A sign: *Home of Winnie.* A statue of a bear cub in a tree, the real bear that inspired Winnie-the-Pooh. A cluster of kids and a frazzled teacher.

**TEACHER:** Someone stole Winnie's scarf! The kids knitted it!

**\[PLAY\] Small mystery: "Winnie's Scarf."**

- **Clue 1:** Red wool fibres on the bark of the statue's tree.
- **Clue 2:** Bird droppings and twigs under the gas station sign across the road.
- **Clue 3:** Through Mani's binoculars: a crow's nest on the sign, with a flash of red wool.

The culprit is a crow. Preethi climbs the ladder from the gas station, gets the scarf back, and is attacked by a very angry crow. The kids cheer. Mani films it for Nila.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** Anni lost a fight to a crow. Best day of my life.

At the gas pumps, a trucker with a grey beard and a CB radio: **BIG LOU**.

**BIG LOU:** You folks headed west? Two black SUVs been running Highway 17 all week. No front plates. They stop where nobody stops. (He spits.) You hear "Kane" on channel nineteen, you get off the road.

**SCENE 3 — EXT. NIPIGON RIVER BRIDGE / LAKE SUPERIOR — DAY 3, AFTERNOON**

The cable-stayed bridge over the Nipigon River. Superior's frozen edge. At sunset, the Sleeping Giant on the horizon across the bay at Thunder Bay. Preethi asks Mani to stop the car. They stand in the cold and watch it.

**PREETHI:** Nee bayappadriya? (Are you scared?)

**MANI:** Romba. (Very.) (beat) You?

**PREETHI:** I'm a pharmacist, Mani. Last month my biggest problem was a doctor's handwriting.

**SCENE 4 — INT. DR. OKAFOR'S HOUSE, THUNDER BAY — NIGHT 3**

A warm house full of books. **DR. GRACE OKAFOR**, 60, Nila's former criminology professor. Jollof rice and tea. Nila is on speaker.

**DR. OKAFOR:** Nila was the best student I ever had, and she chose radio. I've never forgiven her.

**LIGHTHOUSE:** You came to my first show.

**DR. OKAFOR:** I came to judge it. (To Mani and Preethi.) Tell me about the man.

**\[PLAY\] Profile-building.** Mani and Preethi share what they know; Nila and Okafor build the profile together. The player picks traits from evidence:

- *Collects rare, dangerous animals* → **a collector who needs control.**
- *Talks to Nila on the radio for months* → **a voyeur who needs an audience.**
- *Sends gifts and messages* → **he wants to be seen as generous, even while killing.**
- *Killed Victor at 600 metres* → **patient, practised, never in a hurry.**

**DR. OKAFOR:** He won't kill you from a distance, Mani. He'll want you in the room. He'll want to watch you choose. (She writes a name on a card.) Constable Rachel Dunn, OPP, Red Lake. She's honest. Too honest for her own good.

**SCENE 5 — EXT. HIGHWAY 17, NEAR IGNACE — DAY 4, 14:00. SET PIECE: THE AMBUSH**

Whiteout. Visibility ten metres. A transport truck jackknifed across both lanes, hazard lights blinking.

**BIG LOU (ON CB):** …whoever's westbound by Ignace, that's no accident, I just came through—

Two black SUVs slide out of the snow behind them. Doors open. Men in white parkas with rifles.

**KANE (ON THEIR RADIO, CALM):** Mani. Just the file. Nobody has to freeze tonight.

**\[PLAY\] First gunfight of Part 2.** The game teaches both styles.

- **Stealth:** **\[PREETHI\]** slips into the ditch with the shotgun and flanks through the spruce. **\[MANI\]** draws attention with the car horn. Quiet takedowns from behind in the whiteout.
- **Aggressive:** M4 from behind the engine block, Desert Eagles, suppress and push.

Three waves: the SUV men, a man on the truck's roof, and the driver of the second SUV trying to flee. Ivy's car takes bullets; Ivy is unhurt but shaking.

**\[PLAY\]** Search the SUV. In the back: a hard case stencilled *NSB*. Inside, six fragmentation grenades in foam. **First grenade case** → car trunk. Each character takes two.

**PREETHI** (staring at it): These are grenades, Mani. Real grenades.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** Then they're your grenades now. Put them in the trunk, Anni.

**SCENE 6 — INT. MOTEL, DRYDEN — NIGHT 4 / DAY 5, MORNING**

A motel by the highway. Preethi patches a graze on Mani's arm. Neither sleeps much.

Morning. The car won't start. Fuel on the snow under the engine.

**\[PLAY\] Small mystery: "The Fuel Line."**

- **Clue 1:** Fresh boot prints from the motel office door to the car and back.
- **Clue 2:** The night clerk, **TYLER**, 20s, in brand-new winter boots, with $200 in crisp bills in his tip cup.

**\[PLAY\] Choice:** report Tyler to the OPP, or flip him.

**SCENE 8 — EXT. RED LAKE — DAY 5, DUSK**

## Episode 3 · Gold Country

The investigation begins. Ortiz drives to Windsor to guard Nila. In Red Lake, Preethi finds Nila's poison in four dead miners' blood, Mani traces the Hayden family through the museum and a mislabelled rock, and the town's honest cop and a bush pilot enter the story. Then Nila's voice stops mid-sentence.

**SCENE 1 — INT. NORTHERN STAR MOTEL, ROOM 7 — DAY 6, 06:00**

Mani hasn't slept. The radio on the bed. Ortiz's voice on it, from the road.

**ORTIZ (ON RADIO):** Took three days off. I'll sit outside her door in Windsor till you come home. Nobody brings her flowers but family. (beat) That's not a favour, Mani. I owe her.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** Sam's here. He brought Tim—sorry, Tom Portons. Okay. Plan for today, Anna. Hospital, museum, rocks.

**SCENE 2 — INT. RED LAKE MARGARET COCHENOUR MEMORIAL HOSPITAL — 09:00**

A small, clean hospital. Snow piled at the doors. **\[PREETHI\]** introduces herself as a visiting hospital pharmacist from Windsor, which is true, and asks for the pharmacy director. She gets **DR. HELEN MARCHETTI**, 52, sharp, tired, a stethoscope like a necklace.

**DR. MARCHETTI:** Windsor? You drove all this way to ask about my formulary?

**PREETHI:** I drove all this way because my sister-in-law has a cancer nobody understands. (She hands over Nila's blood panel.) This compound. Have you seen it?

Marchetti reads it. Closes her office door.

**DR. MARCHETTI:** Four times. Four men who did contract work at the Hayden Deep. All dead within five months. I sent samples to Winnipeg. The results came back "lost". Then Northstar donated a CT scanner to this hospital. (beat) I was told to stop asking.

**\[PLAY\] Puzzle: the five blood panels.** Preethi lays Nila's panel beside the four dead men's. Compare markers line by line and highlight what all five share. The answer: one unnamed peptide, and the same three-week pattern of rising liver enzymes.

**PREETHI:** Same compound. Same pattern. Different start dates. (beat) Nila's three weeks behind the last man.

**DR. MARCHETTI** (quietly): Then you have about three months. Maybe less.

**SCENE 3 — INT. RED LAKE REGIONAL HERITAGE CENTRE — 11:00**

**\[MANI\]** Display cases: gold pans, Norseman models, the 1926 gold rush, mine photographs. A volunteer docent, **MRS. LINDQVIST**, 70s, delighted to have a visitor.

**\[PLAY\] Investigation: the Hayden family.** Four exhibits to examine:

- **A 1934 photo:** two prospectors grinning over a claim post. *"Hayden brothers, Hayden Deep claim."*
- **A mine plaque:** *"Hayden Deep Mine, 1946–1991. At its peak, 300 men."*
- **A newspaper clipping, 1974:** *"Engineer R. Hayden leaves for southern Ontario dam project."* Mani recognises the name from the Greyhaven archive photo.
- **A donor wall:** *"Northstar Biologics — Mark Hayden, 2015."*

**MRS. LINDQVIST:** Mark came home in 2015. Bought the old mine, built a big house way out past the Burn. Nobody's ever been invited. (beat) His father too, they say. Though old Robert must be ninety if he's a day.

Mani writes down "Robert". *(Part 3 seed.)*

**SCENE 4 — EXT. DICKENSON MINE VIEWING LOT, BALMERTOWN — 13:00**

A lot overlooking the mine buildings and headframe, painted long ago by A.Y. Jackson. Mani and Preethi eat Amma's last puliyodharai in the car, heater running.

**PREETHI:** Three months.

**MANI:** Less.

**PREETHI:** What if the cure doesn't exist?

**MANI:** Then Hades wouldn't send presents. You don't brag about a lock unless you have the key.

**SCENE 5 — INT. BALMERTOWN MUNICIPAL OFFICE, ERLE CRULL COLLECTION — 14:00**

Cases of minerals: thousands of specimens, quartz, pyrite, real gold glinting under the lights.

**\[PLAY\] Puzzle: the mislabelled rock.** Find the Hayden Deep specimens (sort by the claim names on the labels). One gold specimen is labelled *"Hayden Deep, Level 6, 1962"*. With the case's magnifier, Mani sees a second, older tag stuck underneath: *"LEVEL 9"*.

**MANI:** The public mine maps show six levels.

**LIGHTHOUSE (ON RADIO):** So there are three more nobody talks about. That's where the lab is, Anna.

**SCENE 6 — INT. OPP DETACHMENT, RED LAKE — 15:30**

A call from the front desk brings them in. **CONSTABLE RACHEL DUNN**, 33, ponytail, notebook, very direct.

**DUNN:** Two people from Windsor, long gun cases, asking questions about Northstar at the hospital and the museum. You know what that sounds like to me?

**\[PLAY\] Interview (dialogue duel).** Mani must not admit the guns are illegal. Good answers: Dr. Okafor's name; the truth about Nila's illness; the GH-01 delivery note. Bad answers: anything about the Ignace ambush.

**DUNN** (after the right answers): Grace Okafor taught me at Lakehead. (She closes her notebook.) I can't help you. I also can't arrest you for being sad. Go home, Mr. Mani. Northstar pays half this town's wages.

**SCENE 7 — EXT. FLOATPLANE BASE, RED LAKE — 16:30**

Planes on skis on the frozen bay. A man in a heavy parka works on the engine of an old Norseman. **DANIEL KEEPER**, 45.
