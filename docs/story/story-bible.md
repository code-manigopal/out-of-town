# Out of Town — Story Bible

## Series
- **Out of Town** is a story-driven thriller series. Each part is named after its place.
- Part 1: **Greyhaven** (8 episodes, 5 endings). Part 2: **Red Lake** (8 episodes, two playable characters). Part 3: hooked (Robert Hayden).
- PC, Steam. A browser prototype of both parts exists (`prototype/browser`). The 3D game is built in Unreal Engine 5.
- Family dialogue (Mani, Nila, Amma, Appa, Preethi) is in **Tanglish**. Every line shows an English subtitle.

## Characters
- **Mani** — 29 (Part 1) / 30 (Part 2). From Vellore. Ex team lead at Auragle; data analytics master's, University of Windsor. Careful, dry. Player. Never kills in Part 1.
- **Nila** — 25 / 26. Younger sister. Criminology degree; chose radio: host of *After Hours*, Lake Merrow 94.1 FM. Quick, funny, sharp. Part 2: handler on radio, call sign **Lighthouse**. She plans the illegal gun buy.
- **Preethi** — 27. Amma's proposed match in Part 1; Mani's wife in Part 2. Hospital pharmacist, ex air-rifle shooter. Calm, precise. Call sign **Anni**.
- **Amma and Appa** — parents in Vellore. Calls only in Part 1; fly in for Part 2.
- **Victor Kessler** — Part 1 villain. Fentanyl lab and exotic-animal smuggling (golden lanceheads from Snake Island; his snake is Midas). Shot dead by Hades in every Part 1 ending.
- **Hades = Mark Hayden** — the man in the long coat who listens. Never connected to his name on screen in Part 1. Part 2 villain; falls into the Smoke, no body. Son of **Robert Hayden** (1978 dam photo), who is alive: Part 3.
- Part 1 allies: Ruth Vane, Tess / Eli / Walter Marsh, Gus Pell, Deputy Ortiz, Dr. Rao, Father Reid, Jonah Pike. Corrupt: Sheriff Corbin. Station manager: Brian Holt.
- Part 2: Kane (security chief, dies in Ep 7), Ivy Laurent, Dr. Marchetti, Daniel Keeper, Bev Arsenault, Sully O'Rourke, Constable Dunn, Rocco Delisle (gun seller under the Ambassador Bridge).

## Part 1 — Greyhaven
Tuesday to Diwali Sunday in early November. Mani drives from Windsor (Tom Portons stops, small mysteries, a chance meeting with Victor) to find Nila missing. The closed paper mill hides the lab and the animals; the barrels go into the lake over the drowned village of Old Merrow.

Endings: **Daybreak** (true), **Out of the Fog**, **Wrong Hands**, **Silent Water**, **Lamplight** (hidden). All leave Nila alive and exposed (lake dive, Pike Point water bottles), Victor shot by Hades, Hades escaped, the siblings home in Windsor.

## Part 2 — Red Lake
Eleven months later. Nila vomits blood on air; a fast "atypical" cancer: Hades's **HX-7**, in the Pike Point water. Guns are bought illegally from Rocco Delisle (Nila's plan); grenades are only taken from enemies. Five-day drive (motels in Sudbury, Wawa, Dryden; Thunder Bay). Ep 3–6 investigation in Red Lake. Ep 7 the Burn: stealth or aggressive combat, both characters fight. Ep 8 the Hades Mansion: tiger, lanceheads, mamba, crocodiles; three-vial puzzle; the cure HX-7C; Pongal ending.

**Part 3 hook:** Preethi is pregnant. A radio voice: "Congratulations, Anni." Robert Hayden beside a burned Mark.
