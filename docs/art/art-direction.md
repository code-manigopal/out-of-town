# Art direction

## Overall
Grounded realism, filmic. Think late-night radio, wet asphalt, sodium lamps, breath in cold air. Handheld-feeling camera in tense scenes, locked-off wide shots for dread. Film grain, slight chromatic aberration, no over-saturated "game" colours.

## Part 1 — Greyhaven
- **Palette:** sodium orange, fog grey, lake teal-black, rust. Warm interiors (motel, diner, Diwali lamps) against cold exteriors.
- **Weather:** dense volumetric fog, drizzle, early November, leafless trees.
- **Light motifs:** Nila's diyas, the red light on the water tower (the watcher), the radio tower beacon at Pike Point.
- **Places:** small-town Ontario main street, a 1970s paper mill, a drowned village under green water, a lonely radio tower.

## Part 2 — Red Lake
- **Palette:** icy blue, bone white, black spruce green, mine-lamp yellow, ember red near the Smoke.
- **Weather:** snow, −30 °C, low winter sun, blizzards in the Burn, steam from the Smoke.
- **Light motifs:** headlights on Highway 17, the Norseman floatplane, the mansion's glass conservatories glowing in the dark forest.
- **Places:** northern highway motels, a gold-mining town, ice roads, a ghost town, a dense burned-and-regrown forest, a stone-and-glass mansion.

## Characters
Realistic, modern, everyday clothes. Characters built in Character Creator 4 (or Blender) and exported as glTF, customised to the character sheets. South Indian features for the family, accurate and respectful. No glamour lighting: people look tired, cold, scared.

## UI
Minimal, diegetic where possible: phone screen, radio dial, Nila's notebook as the journal. Subtitles clean, with speaker name and a small channel icon (radio, phone, tape).

## References (mood only)
*Alan Wake 2* (lighting, Bright Falls), *Hitman* (sandbox readability), *Mare of Easttown*, *Fargo* (small-town crime), *Wind River* (snow thriller), *True Detective* S4 (cold dark north).
