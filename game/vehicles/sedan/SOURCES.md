# Sedan: sources

- `sedan.glb`, `traffic_sedan.glb`, `traffic_hatchback.glb`, `traffic_suv.glb` are generated by
  `tools/blender/build_sedan.py` (Blender 5.2, headless). Every shape is procedural and original: a generic mid-size
  sedan with no badges, logos or real crests. The plate reads "OOT 2026" with a generic "ONTARIO" word and no crest.
- The Blender scene is `art_source/vehicles/sedan/sedan.blend`.
- Audio: the engine, door, trunk, ignition and chime sounds are the project's own generated files in
  `game/audio/sfx/exterior/` (see `gen_exterior_sfx.sh` there). No third-party sounds (SFXMint, Kenney or other) have
  been downloaded for the car.
